# Investara  -  Personal Finance Reimagined 
I just built Investara, a zero-dependency personal finance &amp; compensation dashboard designed to run both as a desktop/mobile PWA (index.html) and as a single self-contained HTML file (investara-ios.html) you can save directly to your iPhone/iPad Files app and use offline.

What It Does


Multi-Profile Budgeting: Switch between household or career profiles, clone scenarios, or create custom profiles persisted in localStorage.
Visual Salary & Budget Analytics: Interactive 12-month cashflow/savings bar chart, 40-bar allocation breakdown (Taxes, Needs, Wants, Savings & 401k), watchlist table, and AI Budget Copilot insights.
Complete Paycheck & 50-State Tax Calculator: Supports Salary and Hourly + 1.5× Overtime modes, 2025/2026 Federal brackets, all 50 US states + DC (+ optional NYC local tax), FICA, Traditional/Roth 401(k), HSA, FSA, and W-4 extra withholding.
Raise & Wealth Compounding Calculator: Model % or flat $ raises, see real inflation-adjusted purchasing power after tax drag, and project 10-year compounded wealth.
Indeed & Glassdoor Salary Explorer: Compare your pay against 46 roles across 26 US metro hubs and 5 seniority tiers with P10–P90 percentiles and direct portal links.
Downloadable High-Res JPEG Budgets: Export a 1920×1200 Investara Budget Blueprint .jpg in 1 click (with native iOS Share Sheet support to save directly to Photos).
Mobile & iOS Ready: Responsive iPhone layout with frosted bottom tab bar, safe-area support, and 1-click .html download.
